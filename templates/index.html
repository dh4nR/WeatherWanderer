    About This Tool

    This application helps you plan your trip by analyzing the 7-day weather forecast for your destination and ranking activities based on weather suitability.

    How It Works
    • Weather data is obtained from the Open-Meteo API
    • Algorithm analyzes temperature, precipitation, and snowfall
    • Activities are scored on a scale of 0-10 (higher is better)
    • Rankings are presented to help you plan your trip
    • Search history is saved in a database for easy reference
